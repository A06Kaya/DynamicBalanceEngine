🛡️ Dynamic Balance Security Engine

Dynamic Balance Engine (DBE) is an advanced, behavior-based security system that replaces static permissions with a living Risk Score (0-100).


📊 Risk Tiers

  • 0-20 (Low Risk): Trusted User. Full access to all features.
  • 21-60 (Medium Risk): Monitoring Mode. You are watched closely.
  • 61-90 (High Risk): Restricted Access. You cannot post or comment.
  • 91-100 (Critical): Account Suspended. Contact administration.

⚔️ Active Defense Systems

🚫 Injection Hunter

Blocks SQL Injection (e.g., ' OR '1'='1) and XSS attacks instantly. Detection sets Risk Score to 95+ immediately.

📢 Smart Spam Filter

Analyzes content for CAPSLOCK abuse (>60%) and unauthorized advertising links. Penalizes with +10 to +15 points.

🌊 Flood Control

Prevents bot activity. Posting more than 5 times in 1 minute triggers an exponential risk penalty.

🕵️ Session Guardian

Locks risk if your IP Address or Browser changes mid-session, preventing Session Hijacking.

❤️ Time Decay (Healing)

Everyone makes mistakes. The system features a Lazy Decay mechanism. Your Risk Score automatically decreases by 1 point for every minute of inactivity or safe behavior. To heal your score, simply take a break.
